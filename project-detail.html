Residential Case Study

The Luxury Dining Room Transformation

A masterclass in modern dining elegance, fusing custom marble fabrication with ambient lighting.

Client Private Residence
Location Lekki Phase 1, Lagos
Timeline 6 Weeks
Scope Interior Styling & Custom Millwork

Client Requirements

The homeowner desired an opulent yet intimate dining environment capable of hosting family gatherings and formal corporate dinner parties. Key requests included an eight-seater bespoke marble table, hidden smart lighting, and acoustic dampening panels.

Design Challenge

The original dining hall suffered from awkward natural lighting shadows and a narrow floor plan that limited movement around standard large dining sets. We needed to maximize visual breadth without sacrificing cozy intimacy.

Our Solution

We engineered a custom-tailored pedestal marble dining table paired with slim-profile velvet upholstered seating. Floor-to-ceiling smoked mirrors were integrated to visually double the room's width, complemented by layered warm chandelier zones.
